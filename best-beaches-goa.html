Goa has over 100 beaches — from the famous party sands of Baga and Calangute to the serene coves of Palolem and Agonda. The best way to explore them is with your own self-drive car. Here are the must-visit beaches:

  • Baga & Calangute — North Goa's party capital, water sports, shacks
  • Anjuna — Wednesday flea market, trance parties, cliffside views
  • Vagator & Chapora — Dil Chahta Hai fort, dramatic red cliffs
  • Palolem — South Goa's crescent-shaped paradise, ideal for kayaking
  • Agonda — Serene, calm, perfect for yoga retreats
  • Morjim — Olive Ridley turtle nesting site, quiet north end
