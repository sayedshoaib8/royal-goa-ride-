Security Deposit Refund

The security deposit (₹3,000 for cars, ₹1,500 for bikes) is fully refundable within 48 hours of vehicle return, provided:

  • The vehicle is returned in the same condition as delivered
  • No traffic fines or toll charges are pending
  • Fuel level matches delivery level
  • Return is on time (or early return charges do not apply)

How to Request a Refund

Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 99753 56697 or email info@royalgoaride.com with your booking details.

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days via the original payment method (UPI, bank transfer, or cash).
